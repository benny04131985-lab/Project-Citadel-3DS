MAINLOOP V16.1 PATCH

The installer inserts this immediately after the first pump_events(); call:

#if defined(__3DS__) || defined(_3DS)
        /*
         * PROJECT CITADEL V16.1: stop immediately after SDL system-close
         * request.
         */
        if (!gPlayingGame)
            break;
#endif

This prevents the current gameplay/menu loop and another GPU presentation
from running after SDL's 3DS backend reports aptMainLoop() == false.
