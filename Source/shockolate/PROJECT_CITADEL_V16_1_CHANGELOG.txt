PROJECT CITADEL 3DS V16.1 — CHANGELOG

Stability
- Stops the engine loop immediately after the 3DS app lifecycle emits
  SDL_QUIT.
- Adds explicit ordered shutdown for music, Citro2D/Citro3D, and SDL.
- Forces process completion only after cleanup on HOME Menu close.
- Uses sdmc:/3ds/SystemShock as the stable working directory for 3DSX/CIA.

Presentation
- Corrects both lower MFD crops from Y=312/H=156 to Y=320/H=160.
- Keeps the V16 640x400 default, full-resolution transport, RGBA8 output fix,
  branded splash, split layout, and black paused/menu lower screen.

Controls
- A: freelook toggle.
- R: activate / left mouse.
- L: fire / right mouse.
- B: exit freelook and center the cursor on the top world view.
- X/Y, START, SELECT, Circle Pad, C-Stick, and touch behavior otherwise remain
  as validated in V16.

Packaging
- Adds Homebrew Menu icon assets.
- Adds a HOME Menu banner asset.
- Adds a branded Project_Citadel_3DS.3dsx output.
- Adds an optional code-only Project_Citadel_3DS.cia target.
- No original game data or res folder is bundled.
